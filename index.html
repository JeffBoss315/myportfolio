Who I Am

I'm Geoffrey Njuguna — Jeff to most people. A Computer Science graduate from Thika Technical Training Institute who builds websites, desktop software and AI systems for real users, usually the kind who have never had a digital tool for the job before.

My Vision

To become one of Africa's leading software engineers by building technology that genuinely changes how businesses, schools and clinics work day to day — not just software that demos well.

How I Work

I start with the problem rather than the stack, ship something usable early, and keep improving it with the people who actually use it. Every project below began as somebody's manual, repetitive process.
